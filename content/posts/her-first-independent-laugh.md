Our daughter is fascinated with our cat, an Orange Tabby with swirls like Cinnabons. She’s always looking for him, always watching him when he’s around, and always wants to know what he’s doing.

As part of their (one-sided) relationship, she shares the end of her play mat with him, where he naps until woken up by her. She rolls and rolls until [she crashes into him](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/uPtBWTMmXF0) and then awkwardly stares at him till he leaves. Thankfully, he’s been nice to her (except that one time she kicked him and he tried to bite her) and let her have a few firsts, such as:

-   Petting him
-   Grabbing a fistful of fur (!)
-   Watching him eat
-   Laughing independently

The laugh happened on the play mat. She was practicing tummy time by holding her neck and body up with outstretched arms, when he came across the mat and stopped at the end of it near the couch. Suddenly she started studying his every move. Then he sneezed in the way that only a cat does by snapping its head, and she thought it was *hi-lari-ous*! She laughed so hard, so wide that her gums showed, her eyes shut, and my wife could hear her in the kitchen.

It was brief but fascinating to see her decide unprompted what’s funny. Until that moment, our daughter was laughing with us based on our facial expressions, like smiling while speaking silly, but this time she showed her budding independence.
