Our daughter is days away from becoming five months old, and it’s been filled with rapid development.

In particular, in the last couple of weeks, my wife and I have noticed that our daughter is developing her personality traits and interests. These traits and interests are sustained and repeated by our daughter over time, so we feel like they are parts of her at this time.

### Personality

-   **Active**: She loves to roll onto her belly and hold her head up as long as humanly possible.
-   **Smiley**: 2 AM? Smiling. 7 AM? Smiling. 2 PM? Smiling. 7 PM? Smiling (mostly).
-   **Curious**: Her head is always rotating, clocking what’s happening, whether it’s the cat passing by, the tv show playing, the flower budding, or the bird flying among the trees.
-   **Persistent**: When she was rolling onto her belly but didn’t have the strength for a returning roll, we flipped her onto her back. And we did it every hour of every night because she would flip back each time. She won the futile battle.
-   **Stubborn**: When nurses attempted to take pictures of her while she was in the womb (!), they were left so frustrated that we received an email reading, “Your daughter is very uncooperative.”
-   **Sensitive**: She’s sensitive to loud sounds *indoors*, causing her to blink comically fast. But that 18-wheeler rattling and screeching by her stroller during an outdoor walk? Meditative.

### Interests

-   **Bright colors**: She loves bright colors, especially yellow and green, which she’s always looking for.
-   **Patterns**: The patterns on our throw pillows? She tries to take them off. You read that right.
-   **Bosnian**: She’s engaged during conversations and readings (*Crvenkapa* fan) in Bosnian, and has started to react the same way to three statements (*Hoćeš mlijeko? Jesi li kakila? Voli te, Tata!*), which indicates she’s learning them.
-   **Squishmallow**: A rainbow lamb Squishmallow named Lana. Our daughter hugs it and then [suplexes](https://youtube.com/shorts/BCh9FHrWo9c?si=pRt3JiIyMDk07h6s) it.
-   **Music**: Bosnian folk. Japanese jazz. Mozart (for babies). Toy piano.
-   **Naps**: In nature. A breeze is preferred. Less excited about indoor naps. Maybe she’s Scandinavian deep down?
-   **Surprises**: Peekaboo doesn’t do. But pretend that you’re walking away and then suddenly turn back as if you’re on a runway? She will belly laugh!
-   **Soccer**: Long concentration. Lots of opinions. None intelligible.
-   **Cats**: Intrigued by our cat in a pull-fistful-fur way. Oh, she loves the way he sneezes too.
-   **Crotchet**: Our home has a few crotchet tapestries hanging on the walls, and she stares at their patterns incessantly.
