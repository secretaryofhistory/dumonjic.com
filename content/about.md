I’m a content designer, storyteller, and essayist who applies storytelling frameworks to technical writing and UX writing.

I was the first and only Technical Writer hired by GoGuardian, where I created the role, built documentation workflows, and proposed a companywide “Culture of Documentation.”

Outside of work, I publish [*The Periplous*](https://theperiplous.com), a library of storytelling techniques for writers to learn from and apply to their stories. I write micro-essays about fatherhood on Sundays in [*Memoirs Beyond the Womb*](/memoirs-beyond-the-womb/), and a monthly missive called [*Velvet Vowel*](https://velvetvowel.substack.com/).

You can find my [resume](/resume/) here, or reach out on the [contact page](/contact/).
