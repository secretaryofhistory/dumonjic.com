This page is home to my musings in various written forms, including essays, [sportswriting](/2025/08/31/sportswriting/), and storytelling analyses published on [The Periplous](https://theperiplous.com).

My primary writing form is the essay. Inspired by Michel de Montaigne, François-René de Chateaubriand, and E.B. White, the essays consist of ~500 words that focus on a single idea, topic, or observation.

The essays include a collection called [*Memoirs Beyond the Womb*](/memoirs-beyond-the-womb/), which focuses on my experience as a first-time dad and watching my daughter experience everything for the first time.
